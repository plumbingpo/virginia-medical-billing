2

AdvancedMD

AdvancedMD offers a comprehensive suite of cloud-based solutions for medical practices, including practice management, electronic health records (EHR), and medical billing services. They aim to streamline operations and improve financial performance for healthcare providers.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Specialty Practices (e.g., Cardiology, Dermatology, Gastroenterology, Orthopedics, Mental Health), Urgent Care centers.

Pricing Model: Typically a percentage of collections (estimated 4-8%), with potential per-claim fees for certain services or flat fees for specific modules.

  • ✅ Integrated EHR and Practice Management system for seamless workflow.
  • ✅ Strong reporting and analytics tools to track financial performance.
  • ⚠️ Can be a more expensive option, especially for smaller practices.
3

Experian Health (Medical Billing Services)

Experian Health provides a range of healthcare revenue cycle management solutions, including medical billing services. They leverage advanced data analytics and technology to optimize claims processing, reduce denials, and accelerate payments for providers.

Specialties Served: Hospitals, Large Physician Groups, Specialty Clinics (e.g., Oncology, Radiology, Pathology), Ambulatory Surgery Centers.

Pricing Model: Primarily a percentage of collections (estimated 3-7%), with customized pricing based on the scope of services and practice size.

  • ✅ Leverages Experian's vast data and analytics capabilities for denial prevention.
  • ✅ Strong focus on compliance and regulatory adherence.
  • ⚠️ May be more geared towards larger organizations, potentially less flexible for very small practices.
4

Coronis Health

Coronis Health is a leading national revenue cycle management company offering end-to-end medical billing solutions. They focus on maximizing reimbursement and improving practice efficiency through a combination of technology and expert billing professionals.

Specialties Served: Anesthesia, Pathology, Radiology, Emergency Medicine, Urgent Care, Hospital-Based Physician Groups, Ambulatory Surgery Centers, Mental Health.

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 4-9%), with variations depending on specialty and service level.

  • ✅ Deep expertise in complex and high-volume specialties.
  • ✅ Dedicated account managers and strong client support.
  • ⚠️ Pricing can be higher due to specialized expertise.
5

Medical Billing Wholesalers

Medical Billing Wholesalers provides comprehensive medical billing and coding services, often acting as a white-label solution for other RCM companies or directly serving practices. They focus on cost-effective, high-quality billing support.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics, Mental Health, Physical Therapy, Podiatry.

Pricing Model: Often a per-claim model (estimated $2-$5 per claim) or a lower percentage of collections (estimated 3-6%) due to their offshore component.

  • ✅ Cost-effective billing solutions, potentially leading to higher net revenue.
  • ✅ Scalable services that can adapt to practice growth.
  • ⚠️ Communication can sometimes be a challenge due to time zone differences and potential language barriers with offshore teams.
6

RevCycle Partners

RevCycle Partners offers medical billing and revenue cycle management services designed to optimize financial performance for healthcare providers. They emphasize transparency, proactive denial management, and personalized service.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, OB/GYN, Cardiology, Gastroenterology, Urology, Mental Health.

Pricing Model: Primarily a percentage of collections (estimated 5-8%), with potential for customized flat fees for specific services.

  • ✅ Strong focus on transparency and detailed reporting for clients.
  • ✅ Proactive approach to denial management and appeals.
  • ⚠️ May not have the same level of specialized expertise as companies focusing on niche, complex specialties.
7

CureMD Healthcare

CureMD provides an integrated platform including EHR, practice management, and medical billing services. Their solutions are designed to simplify clinical and administrative workflows, ensuring efficient revenue cycle management.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Family Medicine, Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, OB/GYN, Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics, Mental Health.

Pricing Model: Often a percentage of collections (estimated 4-7%), sometimes bundled with their EHR/PM software, or per-provider/user fees.

  • ✅ Fully integrated EHR and practice management system for streamlined operations.
  • ✅ User-friendly interface for both clinical and billing staff.
  • ⚠️ The billing service may be less customizable or specialized compared to standalone billing companies.
